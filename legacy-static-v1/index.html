AI Construction Takeoff Platform

Visual walkthrough of the 11 product screens from the tech design. Every screen is the verbatim Stitch mockup. The Editor screen has been wired with a real OpenSeadragon viewport + Konva polygon-drawing overlay so you can pan, zoom, and draw measurable rooms.

Tip: hit ? on any screen to open the switcher Alt+←/→ to flip screens
How this was built

Mockups generated by Stitch (Google) from a tech-design-grounded prompt. The editor's static placeholder image was swapped with OpenSeadragon for tile-style pan/zoom and Konva for the polygon overlay — matching the architecture in §2.2 and §2.3 of the design doc.

Try the editor
  1. Open the Editor
  2. Press P (Polygon)
  3. Click on the canvas to add vertices
  4. Double-click to close — area is computed in SF
  5. Scroll to zoom; drag to pan
